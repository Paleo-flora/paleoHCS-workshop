Venue Information

Address: Zülpicher Str. 49b, 50674 Cologne, Germany.

Institutional Website & Contact →

How to get there:

The institute is close to Köln Süd and Dasselstraße/Bf Süd, both of which are convenient public-transport stops.

  • From Cologne Central Station (Köln Hbf): take a regional train to Köln Süd (about 7 minutes), then walk a short distance to Zülpicher Str. 49b. You can also take Tram 18 toward Klettenberg/Bonn and get off at Eifelwall, or use Tram 9 toward Sülz and get off at Dasselstraße/Bf Süd.
  • From the Central Bus Station (ZOB): since it is next to Köln Hbf, use the same options from the central station.
  • From Cologne Bonn Airport (CGN): take S13 to Cologne central station, then continue by regional train to Köln Süd or by tram as above; total travel time is around 30 minutes to a little under 45 minutes depending on the connection.

Still worried about getting lost? Additional info:

  • • Useful landmarks for international visitors: The institute is located close to Köln Süd Station, the University of Cologne campus, and the GeoMuseum, all of which are well-known local landmarks and easy to identify on maps or by asking for directions. (GeoMuseum Website)
